SpectraLink is a neuro-symbolic AI support system that integrates a knowledge graph (KG) with an LLM to provide safe, personalized, and evidence-based care guidance for caregivers of individuals with ASD.

It provides personalized behavioral interventions, daily care support, caregiver mental health help and corresponding information.

The system also tracks key outcomes including caregiver stress, intervention consistency, and child behavioral stability.

What it will provide: personalised behavioural intervention strategies, daily care routines, and evidence-based responses derived from the knowledge graph. 

What it will not provide: clinical diagnosis, medical treatment decisions, or crisis emergency intervention

Handling uncertainty

When evidence is insufficient or the query involves high uncertainty, the LLM will return a structured refusal with an explanation and suggest escalation. 

The system includes a clear professional referral pathway
